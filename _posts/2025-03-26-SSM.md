Solid-State Modification (SSM) is an innovative method for modifying polymers in their solid state. It involves heating the polymer under an inert gas flow at temperatures between its glass transition (Tg) and melting point (Tm), enabling controlled chain scission and recombination with functional comonomers. This technique does not require solvents or complete melting, making it energy-efficient and environmentally friendly. SSM allows for the introduction of targeted properties into existing polymers, enhancing their performance and enabling their reuse in advanced applications.
